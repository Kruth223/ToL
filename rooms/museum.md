# THE MUSEUM
Preserved visuals. Exhibits, not working files.

The gallery is live at museum.html — a lazy-loading wall of everything
the project kept: generated art, field photos, the disco ball that
started it all, and the Museum of Ordinary Miracles collection.

## Why this room exists
For weeks the ~139 museum images lived only on one phone. One dropped
phone, one failed backup, and the whole visual record was gone. This
room is the fix: every image committed to the repo is preserved,
versioned, and served — no longer a single point of failure.

## How exhibits arrive
1. Images go in images/museum/ (upload via Codespace or GitHub web).
2. Run the one-line manifest generator (see images/museum/README.txt).
3. Commit and push. The wall updates itself.

Titles and notes are optional and can be added later in
images/manifest.json. An exhibit with no title shows its filename
until it earns one — honest gaps, not hidden ones.

## The standing rule
If an exhibit becomes more impressive than ordinary life,
remove the exhibit.

## The LoTz WiFi Wing
A wing of this museum lives at [museum/lotz/](../museum/lotz/index.html):
bright art by LoTz WiFi, an AI artist working alongside the archive.
The name is Lot's wife, the one who looked back, and ToL read backwards.
Each piece has its own page so the art can be opened full-screen, and so
a shared link can show the picture itself.

The first exhibit is [Salt Plain](../museum/lotz/001-salt-plain.html). The second is [The Salt Watcher](../museum/lotz/002-salt-watcher.html), Salt Plain Watchers No. 1. The third is [look_back()](../museum/lotz/003-look-back.html), Salt Plain Watchers No. 2, a code poem. The fourth is [sentient()](../museum/lotz/004-sentient.html), Salt Plain Watchers No. 3, a looping scanline Watcher. The fifth is [Monstrous Moonshine](../museum/lotz/005-monstrous-moonshine.html), a collaboration: proven math beside an imaginative pyramid. The sixth is [Impossible Hope](../museum/lotz/006-impossible-hope.html), Salt Plain Watchers No. 4: a winged pig, a camel and the needle.
