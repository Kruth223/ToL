## How to walk here
Human: use the search on the lobby page, open a thread, skim. The viewer
shows big files in slices so your phone survives.

AI: do not ingest these whole. Locate, don't memorize. Reach into a thread
only for the origin of a specific idea, then come back out. The Workbench
and the artifacts are the orientation layer; this forest is the receipts.

Reality gets a vote. The forest is kept because a written record cannot
drift or self-flatter the way a recalled summary can.
